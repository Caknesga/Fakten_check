    How the demo works

    The extension compares quotes from these prepared reviews with the visible text on the page. Green means supported claims. Yellow means the evidence is unclear, a claim is misleading, or the check is partial. Red means the demo flags a claim as false.

    The ratings cover selected claims. The reports use prepared references, and the page text stays in your browser.
